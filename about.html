001 — Story

From seed to system

It started with small tools for my own work, then for friends, then for teams who needed their busywork to quietly disappear.

Today I design and build AI-assisted workflows end to end: mapping where the real friction is, prototyping fast, and shipping systems that keep working long after launch day.

I care about the details people feel but rarely name: pace, clarity, and the moment something just works.
